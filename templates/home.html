VALUATION EXPLAINED

What determines a property's value?

Property valuation involves examining relevant property characteristics and the conditions affecting a home. Professional appraisals may also consider comparable sales, current market conditions and an inspection of the property.

This project takes a different approach: it uses 13 features from a historical Boston housing dataset to generate a statistical estimate. It does not replace a professional appraisal.
